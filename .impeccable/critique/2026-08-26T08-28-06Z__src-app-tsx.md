## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** This is two products wearing one header, and the header belongs to the weaker one.

The newsroom (`/`) is authored specifically for a Baltic open-data credibility product and could not be dropped into a generic blog. Correspondents carry Baltic lighthouse surnames as declared house style; their avatars are abstract light/signal/current sweeps contractually forbidden from being faces; `TierBadge` and `ProvenanceBlock` turn provenance into the actual aesthetic; `isServable()` makes fail-closed integrity a visible design object. This is provenance-as-craft — the product thesis rendered in UI.

The dashboard (`/data`) is structurally a generic dark analytics template. Every tile is the same object: section → header row → `lg:grid-cols-4` of sparkline KPI cards → `md:grid-cols-2` of comparison charts. `EnergyTile`, `TradeTile`, `LabourTile` and `GovernmentTile` are the same file with different indicator IDs. Three things rescue it, and they are exactly the Baltic-specific instruments: `PowerMarketCard`'s coupled/decoupled congestion signal, `MaritimeTile`/`PortCard`'s sea state and port statistics, and the LV/EE/LT comparison framing. Nothing else on `/data` resists find-and-replace.

The coherence failure is the boundary itself. The newsroom is typographic, restrained, two-weight, emoji-free. The dashboard is emoji-decorated (14 emoji in `EnvironmentTile`, 🔍/📍 as button labels, flag chips) and colour-noisy. Because `SiteLayout` mounts the dashboard chrome above the newsroom, the layout states the strategy backwards: the journalism reads as a tenant inside the dashboard.

**Deterministic scan:** `detect.mjs --json src` → exit 2, **1 finding**: `broken-image` at `src/newsroom/snippet.ts:4`. That is a false positive — the `<img>` is inside a string template, not rendered markup. The near-clean scan is real and earned: 0 raw Tailwind text steps, 0 arbitrary `text-[Npx]`, 0 `font-bold`, 0 `tracking-wider` misuse, and `tests/typography.test.ts` passes 17/17. Typography discipline is genuinely enforced.

The detector's blind spots were covered by mechanical checks and runtime measurement, which found materially more:
- `--text-tertiary` #64748b on `--bg-page` = **4.03:1** (needs 4.5); on `--bg-card` = **3.75:1**.
- `--text-muted` #475569 = **2.53:1** / **2.36:1** — fails even the 3:1 non-text floor.
- **0** `prefers-reduced-motion` blocks against 52 motion usages, including a 30s infinite ticker.
- `EnergyTile`, `GovernmentTile`, `LabourTile`, `TradeTile`: no loading, empty or error state.
- `InsightsBanner` `min-w-[320px]`/`min-w-[280px]` — horizontal overflow risk on small screens.

**Runtime evidence (measured in-page on `/data`, 859px viewport):**
- White text on the accent fill `--news-accent: #38bdf8` = **2.56:1**. This affects the selected country chip, the selected range chip, the onboarding "Next" button and the 🔍/📍 search buttons. Assessment B measured the accent as a *foreground* (6.91:1, passing) and missed that it is used as a *background* behind white text. This is the single most systemic contrast defect and no static scan caught it.
- **43 of 43** interactive elements are under 44×44 CSS px; country and range chips render at 26px tall.
- **49** instances of "No data available", all from one component, `BalticCompareChart.tsx:66-72`, each in a 160px or 256px tall box.
- Heading order is broken: `H2 "Welcome to portaBaltica"` (the tutorial) precedes `H1 "Baltic data"`; port names render as `H2`, siblings of top-level tile sections.
- The section nav overflows the viewport at 859px — "Business" and "Maritime" are clipped with no visible scroll affordance.
- Rendered font sizes were 12/14/16/22/28/40px — all on the named scale, confirming the typography contract holds at runtime.

## Overall Impression

The trust argument is beautifully built and stops at the newsroom door. `/` earns belief structurally rather than asserting it — checks written as judgeable sentences, datasets linked back to publishers, a corrections rate computed and shown even when zero. Then `/data`, which the newsroom explicitly calls "the evidence", presents 8 of 9 tiles of possibly-quarter-old official statistics with no period stamp, no provenance, and an empty state that cannot tell a reader whether a series is down or simply not published for their country.

The single biggest opportunity: **give every dashboard figure the passport the articles already have.** You compute `assumptions[]` server-side and never surface it. That one move would close the credibility gap, and it is the only change that makes `/data` un-clonable.

## What's Working

1. **Provenance-as-aesthetic** (`ProvenanceBlock`, `TierBadge`, `Byline`, `CorrectionsPage`, `isServable`). It works because the trust argument is structural, not asserted: checks are phrased so a reader can judge them ("No number appears in the text that is absent from the data"), the byline is guaranteed to contain "AI correspondent" with no prop to suppress it, and the corrections rate is shown even at zero. The compliance surface is the delight — rare.
2. **Category-true instruments**: `PowerMarketCard`'s coupled/decoupled spread and `MaritimeTile`'s "2 quarters behind" banner encode domain physics — interconnector congestion, official-statistic lag — that a generic dashboard cannot fake.
3. **Typographic discipline that is actually enforced.** The named scale holds at runtime, the test passes 17/17, and policy prose measured 18px/30.6px at 608px — about 68 characters, textbook. Most projects document a scale and drift; this one does not.

## Priority Issues

**[P1] "Back to dashboard" navigates to the news feed.**
`IndicatorPage.tsx:180` and `:191` both call `navigate('/')` under the label "← Back to dashboard"; `/` is the news feed. *Why it matters:* the core verification loop is article → "Check it yourself →" → `/indicator/:id` → back. "Back" strands the user on the front page, breaking the one journey the entire product is built around. *Fix:* `navigate('/data')` or `navigate(-1)`; same bug in `ApiDocsPage.tsx:31`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable clarify`

**[P1] The dashboard is not operable for keyboard, low-vision or touch users.** Four measured defects, one root cause — the a11y work was done in `src/components/news/**` and never crossed into `src/components/**`. White on `--news-accent` #38bdf8 = 2.56:1 on every selected control; `--text-tertiary` 4.03:1 and `--text-muted` 2.53:1 on real informational text; `.news-focus` appears 31× in the newsroom and 0× in the dashboard, so 25 buttons and 11 nav links have no designed focus ring; all 43 interactive elements are under 44×44px, chips at 26px; and 0 `prefers-reduced-motion` guards against a 30s infinite ticker. *Fix:* darken the accent to ~#0369a1 for white-on-fill or switch to dark text on the light accent; raise `--text-tertiary` to ≥#7d8ba1 and stop using `--text-muted` for informational text; apply `.news-focus` globally via `button:focus-visible, a:focus-visible`; raise chip padding to a 44px hit area; add a `prefers-reduced-motion` block.
Suggested command: `/impeccable audit`

**[P1] One empty state, rendered 49 times, that explains nothing.** `BalticCompareChart.tsx:66-72` renders "No data available for {title}" at 12px in 4.03:1 grey inside an `h-64` box. With the API unavailable, `/data` becomes roughly 12,000px of identical grey boxes. It cannot distinguish "upstream is down" from "Eurostat does not publish this for Estonia" from "you picked a range with no observations". *Fix:* differentiate by cause, shrink the empty box to a single row rather than a full-height chart, name the upstream and the period attempted, and offer a retry.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] Each brand hue carries three meanings, and one of them is wrong.** Green = Estonia (`BalticCompareChart`, `PowerMarketCard`) *and* "value rose" (`IndicatorCard.tsx:200`); amber = Lithuania *and* stale/decoupled/warning; blue = Latvia *and* electricity *and* link. Worse, `isPositiveChange = change >= 0` makes rising unemployment, inflation, government debt and producer prices render as a green ▲. On the Economy tile a green-because-Estonia line sits inches from a green-because-up delta. *Fix:* reserve LV/EE/LT hues for country identity only; add a per-indicator `goodDirection` flag so sentiment colour reflects meaning; restore the legend on compact `BalticCompareChart`.
Suggested command: `/impeccable colorize`

**[P1] Only Maritime discloses data age.** `dataFreshness.ts` (`freshnessOf`, "2 quarters behind") is wired solely into `MaritimeTile`. The other eight tiles present Eurostat and PxWeb series — routinely a quarter or more behind — with no period and no as-of. *Why it matters:* undisclosed staleness on 8 of 9 tiles is precisely the credibility gap the newsroom closes and the dashboard reopens, on a product whose entire pitch is traceability. *Fix:* surface `freshnessOf(latestPeriod)` and the period label on `IndicatorCard` and every tile header; the helper is already generic.
Suggested command: `/impeccable harden`

**[P2] Dashboard chrome colonises the newsroom.** `SiteLayout` renders `Header` (11 dashboard tabs), `DataTicker`, and the country/year controls above *every* news route; `NewsroomLayout` then adds a second nav whose "Latest" also points to `/`. Two navs both linking `/`, inert controls while reading, and a numeric ticker competing with the lead story. *Fix:* render `DataTicker` and the filter cluster only on `/data*`, `/indicator`, `/api-docs`; collapse `Header` to wordmark + News/Data + theme on news routes.
Suggested command: `/impeccable layout`

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (impatient power user).** Greeted on first `/data` visit by `OnboardingTutorial` he did not ask for, over a dashboard that has not loaded. To reach "Estonia unemployment" he switches country in a 26px chip, then scrolls past 8 KPI cards and 12 charts — and the green ▲ he finally lands on tells him the wrong emotional story. No `/` search, no command palette, no jump-to-indicator. The `ApiDocsPage` chips that look like shortcuts are `<code onClick>` he cannot tab to. Precise values require a pixel-perfect hover with no keyboard readout.

**Sam (screen reader + keyboard only).** Charts are a wall: every recharts SVG in `IndicatorCard`, `IndicatorChart`, `BalticCompareChart`, `PowerMarketCard` and the `IndicatorTable` sparklines has no `role`, `<title>`, `<desc>` or data-table alternative — the core content of a data product is invisible to him, and compact compare charts encode country by line colour alone. Tabbing the dashboard produces no visible focus ring on any of the 25 buttons. `DataTicker` duplicates its list for the marquee and is not `aria-hidden`, so every value is announced twice. Heading order starts at H2 before H1. Wins worth keeping: the skip link, `aria-label`/`aria-pressed` on country and year buttons, and the whole `.news-focus` treatment — which simply needs to cross into `/data`.

**Ilze (Latvian newspaper journalist verifying a figure before print) — project-specific.** She reads a tier-A story on Baltic house prices; `ProvenanceBlock` gives her dataset, retrieved-at and checks, exactly right. She clicks "Check it yourself →" and lands correctly on `/indicator/house_prices?country=LV` with the article's country carried through. To confirm "fastest in the Baltics" she needs EE and LT — but the compact chart suppresses its legend, so she cannot be certain which line is Latvia. She needs the exact figure and its exact quarter to attribute: the tooltip needs a precise hover, there is no data table, CSV export is "Pro / coming soon", and no tile but Maritime states the statistical period. Then she clicks "← Back to dashboard" and lands on the news feed. Verification stalls at precisely the precision she came for.

## Minor Observations

- Skeleton headers differ from loaded headers in **size and wording** → layout shift: `PropertyTile` "🏗️ Property & Energy" (`text-callout`) vs "Property & energy" (`text-title`). `MaritimeTile` gets this right.
- Three label-casing systems coexist: Title Case, `uppercase tracking-widest`, and sentence case.
- `SystemStatusFooter` exposes "Infrastructure cost /mo", "Revenue", "Phase" and "Moonshot Status" — internal financials on a public credibility product.
- Switching to EE/LT degrades visibly: `BusinessTile`/`PropertyTile` announce "Latvia data only" yet still render three Latvia-only search boxes; `GovernmentTile`'s gas-price card returns `null`, leaving a silent grid hole.
- "Trend (3Y)" is a static label over `series.slice(-12)` — 12 points is not 3 years for monthly or annual series.
- EU-fund statuses render in raw Latvian ("apstiprināts") to an English-only interface.
- `ErrorBoundary.tsx:25-31` uses inline `fontSize` values outside the token system (defensible as a CSS-independent fallback, worth a comment).
- Primary actions are grey, not brand: onboarding "Next" and search buttons use slate; the ocean accent appears for interaction almost only on `ErrorBoundary`'s reload.
- `CHART_TICK_SIZE = 10px` sits below the 12px caption floor.
- The `/data` footer still lists Riga Open Data, a documented dead upstream.

## Questions to Consider

1. Which product is the host and which is the guest? The layout currently states the opposite of the strategy.
2. Could every dashboard number carry the article's passport? You already compute `assumptions[]` server-side — why is it not on the tile?
3. If each brand hue could keep only one meaning, which wins — country or sentiment?
4. Is undisclosed staleness worse than no data for a traceability product?
5. Who is `/data` for? First load optimises for a tourist and taxes the returning analyst every visit.
