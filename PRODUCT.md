# portaBaltica

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Analysts and business decision-makers working with Latvia, Estonia and Lithuania.
The owner confirmed this audience during the September 2026 design exploration.

## Product purpose

Connect Baltic reporting to comparable, inspectable public data. A reader should
be able to move from a finding to its observation period, country and source,
without mistaking a forecast, a missing reading or an earlier observation for
current evidence.

## Positioning and capabilities

Free articles, cross-country comparisons, historical series, exports and feeds.
The Dashboard retains the broad all-sector overview and individual sector tools.
Data explorer is the focused workspace; indicator permalinks open the same tool.
Business briefings include a complete automated public sample with source-linked
prices, labour costs and retail observations. A country-focused planning note
states the dated change, comparison basis, limits and next measurable check.
Research links preserve the selected history, representation and inspected
period; they are live views, not frozen snapshots. Articles expose their
recorded publication observations and a JSON evidence download when available.
Bespoke enquiries remain closed
unless explicitly enabled; there is no established paid subscription.
The commercial boundaries remain in docs/launch-and-revenue-plan.md.

## Brand commitments

The owner requested a full-product reimagination after finding the earlier
Signal Desk migration and spatial homepage too limited in scope. News, article
reading, data exploration and indicator detail must each have a purpose-built
composition, not unchanged internals behind a new entrance.

The light scheme uses a muted slate-blue accent rather than the prototype's
strong cobalt. The dark scheme borrows Baltic Observatory's deep petrol and warm
copper. Story-specific evidence graphics, condensed editorial typography and a consistent
story-to-evidence journey connect the surfaces. An animated entrance must not
be mandatory before using the research tools.

## Product principles

- Offer both a broad sector scan and a focused investigation, clearly named.
- Keep country, period, measurement basis and source beside the evidence.
- Preserve corrections and explicit AI disclosure.
- Keep data and reporting connected, without claiming live charts are the exact
  frozen observations in a previously published article.
- Give the journal an authored evidence reveal; keep analytical motion brief
  and functional, without mandatory entrances or animated numeric values.

## Accessibility and constraints

Keyboard access, visible focus, readable chart labels, reduced motion and mobile
navigation that contains the same destinations as desktop.
No third-party font requests, new tracking, accounts or paid infrastructure for
the redesign. The shared editorial family is self-hosted under its open licence.
Respect existing theme preferences and retain semantic country and status colours.

## Evidence and open decisions

The selected prototype was reviewed on 2026-09-09. Its static data has not been
copied into the application. Actual figures use the existing managed API.
No award, readership, customer or commercial performance outcome is claimed.
