## Design Context

### Users
English-reading people following the Baltics, including analysts and small
business operators. They read news, check its evidence and compare country data
on desktop and mobile.

### Brand Personality
Clear, restrained and precise. Prioritise readable evidence and fast navigation
over decorative chrome.

### Aesthetic Direction
The owner explicitly prefers the existing top News / Overview / Economy
navigation. Preserve its appearance and destinations, the existing light/dark
themes, system-font family and named type/spacing tokens in DESIGN.md.
The approved improvement is simplification, not a new palette or brand.

### Design Principles
1. One primary navigation row; contextual filters must not imitate that row.
2. Search is a compact tool, not a form section competing with the lead story.
3. Controls state their scope and appear where their effects are visible.
4. Loading, missing data and failures are different, recoverable states.
5. Maintain 44px targets, keyboard operation, explicit AI disclosure and source
   provenance while reducing visual clutter.
